# HS Code Categorization

Trade codes used in this analysis fall into three groups: finished grid equipment (Tier 1), supporting equipment for grid monitoring and reactive power (Tier 2), and the upstream materials and components that go into them (Critical Inputs).

## Tier 1: Finished Grid Equipment

The core hardware that moves and controls electricity on the grid.

| HS Code | What It Covers |
|---------|----------------|
| 8504 | Transformers, static converters, and inductors |
| 8535 | Switching and protection equipment, above 1,000V |
| 8536 | Switching and protection equipment, 1,000V or below |
| 8537 | Boards, panels, and consoles for control or distribution |
| 8544 | Insulated wire and cable (e.g., underground and submarine) |
| 7413 | Bare copper conductors (overhead lines) |
| 7614 | Bare aluminum conductors (overhead lines, ACSR) |

## Tier 2: Smart Grid, Monitoring, and Reactive Equipment

Supporting equipment that helps the grid run efficiently and reliably.

| HS Code | What It Covers |
|---------|----------------|
| 8532 | Electrical capacitors (reactive power, power factor correction) |
| 9030 | Instruments for measuring electrical quantities |

## Critical Inputs: Upstream Materials and Components

The raw materials and parts that feed into the manufacturing of Tier 1 equipment.

| HS Code | What It Covers | Used In |
|---------|----------------|---------|
| 7225 | Flat-rolled alloy steel, 600 mm wide or more | Transformer cores (GOES, NOES) |
| 7226 | Flat-rolled alloy steel, less than 600 mm wide | Transformer cores (GOES, NOES) |
| 7308 | Structures and parts of structures, iron or steel | Transmission towers, substation steel |
| 7407 | Copper bars, rods, and profiles | Feedstock for winding wire |
| 7408 | Copper wire | Transformer windings, busbars |
| 7604 | Aluminum bars, rods, and profiles | Conductor rod stock |
| 7605 | Aluminum wire | Drawn wire for stranding |
| 8546 | Electrical insulators (any material) | Porcelain, glass, and composite insulators |
| 8547 | Insulating fittings for electrical machines | Bushings, spacers, insulating parts |

## Notes on Two Codes

**7308 (iron/steel structures)** also covers non-grid items like bridges and building frames. Trade numbers cannot be cleanly isolated to transmission towers and substation structures.

**9030 (electrical measurement instruments)** is a broad category that includes lab and electronics test equipment. Most volume is not grid-specific. Smart grid communications hardware typically falls under different codes (8517, 8471).
